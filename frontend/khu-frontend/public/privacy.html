Privacy policy

KHU Live — unofficial fan app · last updated 20 November 2026

Who we are

KHU Live is an independent fan app that shows Kenya hockey scores, fixtures and tables. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the Kenya Hockey Union. Public match data comes from kenyahockeyunion.org and from volunteer match-day contributors.

What stays on your phone

Your favourite teams, light/dark theme, "already saw the welcome screen", whether you dismissed the install prompt, and a cached copy of the latest tables are saved in your browser's local storage so the app is fast and works with a weak connection. We never receive these, and you can clear them any time in your browser or app settings.

What we store on our server

Only if you turn on match alerts: your device's push-notification address (a technical token supplied by your browser or phone) and the list of teams you follow, so we can send alerts about those teams. Turn alerts off in the app or your phone settings, or ask us to delete it, and it is removed.

Only if you place a shop order: your name, M-Pesa phone number, delivery town and address, any note you write, what you ordered and the M-Pesa receipt. We use them only to take payment, deliver the order, give support and keep order and tax records. The store that sells the item receives your name, phone and address so it can deliver. Payments are handled by Safaricom M-Pesa: we never see your PIN. Shop stores are independent sellers and are not the Kenya Hockey Union. Ask us to delete your order details once your order is complete and any refund or legal record-keeping period has passed.

Our hosting providers (Vercel and Render) keep standard server logs that can include your IP address and device type for security and to limit abuse. We use them for nothing else.

Cookies, analytics and ads (only if you accept)

The first time you open the app we ask whether we may use cookies from two Google services. If you tap Decline, neither is loaded, no Google cookies are set and the app works exactly the same. A browser that sends "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" is treated as having declined, so it is not asked at all.

  • Google Analytics counts visits (which screens are opened, rough device type and region) so we can see what is useful. It uses the cookies _ga and _ga_…, your IP address is anonymised, and Google's advertising features and ad personalisation signals are switched off for it.
  • Google AdSense may show advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar identifiers to show and measure ads, limit how often you see one, prevent fraud and, unless you turn it off, personalise ads. Read how Google does this at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. Google decides how it uses that data; we do not receive it.

We never show ads in the shop, at checkout or on the admin pages. Ads, where they appear, are marked and kept apart from scores and tables.

Change your mind any time: tap Cookie choices at the bottom of any screen to be asked again. Choosing Decline after accepting removes Google's cookies from this site and reloads the app. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings.

What we don't do

  • No profiling by us, and no selling of your data. The only sharing is a shop order's delivery details with the store you bought from, and what Google's two services above collect if you accept them.
  • Our own screen counter uses no cookies and no personal details.
  • No accounts, passwords, location, contacts, camera or microphone for normal users. Shop orders ask for delivery details only when you buy.

Children

The app is a general sports app and is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices & contact

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and object to how it is used. We keep order records only as long as needed for delivery, refunds and tax records, and delete alert tokens when you turn alerts off. To do any of these, email geofryoduor108@gmail.com (the app's developer, Geofry Oduor). If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya (odpc.go.ke). We may update this page; the date at the top shows the latest version.
