Privacy policy

KHU Live — unofficial fan app · last updated 25 October 2026

Who we are

KHU Live is an independent fan app that shows Kenya hockey scores, fixtures and tables. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the Kenya Hockey Union. Public match data comes from kenyahockeyunion.org and from volunteer match-day contributors.

What stays on your phone

Your favourite teams, light/dark theme, "already saw the welcome screen", whether you dismissed the install prompt, and a cached copy of the latest tables are saved in your browser's local storage so the app is fast and works with a weak connection. We never receive these, and you can clear them any time in your browser or app settings.

What we store on our server

Only if you turn on match alerts: your device's push-notification address (a technical token supplied by your browser or phone) and the list of teams you follow, so we can send alerts about those teams. Turn alerts off in the app or your phone settings, or ask us to delete it, and it is removed.

Our hosting providers (Vercel and Render) keep standard server logs that can include your IP address and device type for security and to limit abuse. We use them for nothing else.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad trackers.
  • No analytics or profiling.
  • No accounts, passwords, location, contacts, camera or microphone for normal users.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

Children

The app is a general sports app and is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices & contact

To ask what is stored or to have it deleted, contact the app's developer through github.com/Geoduor. We may update this page; the date at the top shows the latest version.
