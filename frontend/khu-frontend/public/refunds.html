Refund & cancellation policy

KHU Live shop · last updated 24 November 2026

Who sells what

Shop items are sold by independent partner stores. KHU Live passes your order and M-Pesa payment details to the store so it can deliver. We are not the Kenya Hockey Union.

When you get a full refund

You get your money back in full, to the M-Pesa number you paid from, if: the payment went through but the order could not be completed; the store cancels the order or cannot supply the item; or the order was never shipped. The order screen shows "Cancelled. Your refund is being arranged" when this happens. Refunds are usually completed within a few working days.

Payment problems

If the M-Pesa request fails, times out or you cancel it on your phone, you are not charged and the order is not placed. If money left your account but the order shows as failed, contact us with your M-Pesa receipt code and we will check it.

Wrong, damaged or faulty items

If an item arrives damaged, faulty or not as described, contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order details and, if you can, a photo. We will work with the store to replace it or refund you. Your rights under Kenyan consumer law are not affected.

Changed your mind

Because partner stores set their own return rules, returns for change of mind depend on the store. Ask us before you pay if this matters to you and we will tell you the store's rule.

How to reach us

Email geofryoduor108@gmail.com with your order number and the phone number used for payment.
