KHU Live — unofficial fan app

About KHU Live

KHU Live is a free fan app that puts Kenya field hockey in one place: live scores, fixtures, league tables, results, cups, top scorers and team and player pages. It is built for fans, players and families who want the latest results on a phone, even on a weak connection.

It is independent. It is not run by, endorsed by or affiliated with the Kenya Hockey Union.

Where the information comes from

Public match data comes from kenyahockeyunion.org and from volunteer match-day contributors. If a result is wrong or missing, tell us and we will fix it. The Union's own results are the official record.

Logos and copyright

Club, league and Union names, crests and photos belong to their owners. They are shown only to identify teams. If you own one and want it changed or removed, email us and we will act promptly.
