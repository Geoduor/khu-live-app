Player profiles
Add a player's shirt number, position, short bio and photo. These show on the team's Squad list and on the player's own page. A shirt number belongs to one player in a team. To rename a player, pick them from the list and change the name. Only add a photo with the player's permission (a parent or guardian's for anyone under 18). Do not enter dates of birth, addresses or phone numbers.
Import squads from the KHU website
Reads each team's public player list on kenyahockeyunion.org and adds the players, their shirt numbers and the photos KHU has. It only fills gaps: anything you or an agent already entered is never changed, and grey placeholder pictures are ignored. Positions are not on the KHU site, so add those with the form above. It runs in the background and can take 10 to 30 minutes for all leagues, so doing one league at a time is easier.
Player Stats
KHU's site doesn't publish player stats — these are tracked here directly. Re-entering the same player replaces their totals (send the new total, not a delta).

Visitors & usage
Private counts with no cookies and no personal data: each person is counted once per day and cannot be followed across days. People whose browser asks not to be tracked are not counted.
Sponsor slot
A sponsor card shown on the Home, Fixtures and Results screens, always labelled "Sponsored". Views are counted when at least half the card is seen; clicks are counted too, so you can show a sponsor real numbers.
Backup & restore
One file with your agents, manual results, stats and tournaments (including every score entered here or by agents). It contains agent password hashes, so keep it private and never upload it to GitHub.
Off-site backup & season history
A copy goes to your separate storage bucket every night at 02:00 (once the BACKUP_S3_* settings are added on Render). Saved seasons are permanent: once saved, a season cannot be changed or replaced from this page.
Add a past season
Import a finished season from a file (for example data supplied by the KHU). Saved seasons are permanent and cannot be overwritten.
