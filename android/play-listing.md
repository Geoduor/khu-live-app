## Full description
KHU Live brings Kenya hockey to your phone: live scores, upcoming fixtures, league tables, top scorers,
team pages and tournament brackets — all in one fast app.

• Live and recent results with match details
• Fixtures for the Premier, Super and National leagues (men and women)
• League tables with form guides
• Top scorers and team profiles
• Tournaments and cups, including the Nyota Hockey Fest
• Follow your favourite teams and get match alerts
• Share score cards with friends in one tap
• Works on slow connections and keeps the last data when you're offline

Unofficial fan app. KHU Live is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the Kenya Hockey Union.
Data is sourced from the public Kenya Hockey Union website (kenyahockeyunion.org) and entered by volunteer
match-day contributors.

## Trademark note
The app shows the Kenya Hockey Union name and crest. To avoid a trademark/impersonation rejection keep
"Unofficial fan app" in the title area or description, and ideally get written permission from KHU
(attach it if Google asks). If permission is refused, publish under a neutral name (e.g. "Hockey Kenya Live")
and replace the crest in the icon.
